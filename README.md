# MDS608-group-9
Consequences of the Decision "Basic with Ads" has both positive and negative
outcomes:
• Positive Outcomes:
o
This plan make the subscription affordable for the user who can’t pay for
the premium subscription. Now the more people will use the Netflix and
make it more popular in the world. It will increase the popularity of
Netflix.
o Now revenue also start generating from ads (The company whose ads run
on Netflix will pay for those ads to Netflix) . Large amount of revenue
start generating from ads.
o
Subscriber retention also improved. The subscriber who stopped
subscription due to price , again started getting subscription plans due to
Basic ad plan.
• Negative Outcomes:
o
Premium users starts showing dissatisfaction because of ads
interruption. Users start loosing feeling of premium lifestyle.
o
It can be risk for the premium brand image of Netflix. Because Netflix
image is like a premium brand people buy subscription as it is a premium
brand. The basic ad plan can destroy this image of Netflix.
o In ad supported plans the content quality is not much great so it will
make the basic ad plan user experience bad on Netflix. If the content
quality will low people will not subscribe .
Alternative Decision and Changes If placed in the decision-maker's position, I
would consider the following adjustments to optimize the "Basic with Ads" plan:
• Dynamic Ad Placement: Dynamic ads will be shown to the user. The ads
shown to the users according to his or her interest.
• Enhanced Value Proposition: To the partner services I will provide ad-
supported content. This will help in growing revenue from ads.
• User-Centric Testing: I will first launch the beta version of this plan to see how
it is impacting Netflix . If impacts are bad. We will not launch alpha version. Or
and improved version will be launched. If the impacts are good then we will
launch the final version.
• AI-Powered Ad Recommendations: Make a team to build AI models which will
give the user ads according to the content they see. This model will analyze the
content user see and then use algorithms on that data and then predict which ad
will be better for the user.
Recommendations for Future Improvements
• Monitor User Satisfaction: To know user satisfaction build tools which will
survey and analyze ad frequency, user frustration level.
• Advertiser Transparency: For building trust share number of click- through,
viewer engagement rate to the advertising partners.
• Scalable Ad Models: Offer different tiers with different price in ad- supported
plans in which ad frequency will vary. The plan which has low price will have
higher frequency of ad , the plan with higher price have less frequency of ads. It
means if you pay high price your experience will be better, if you pay less price
you have to watch ads more frequently.
• Content Exclusivity: Provide exclusive content partnerships or early access to
famous shows for ad-supported users. Because of this people will start buying
basic ads plan.
• Ad-Free Premium Incentives: Keep a clear difference between ad- supported
users and premium user. So that premium users don’t have to see ads they can
buy premium subscription and avoid ads
